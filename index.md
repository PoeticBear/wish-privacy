# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: September 10, 2026**

Wish respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

## 1. Information We Collect

Wish is designed to minimize the collection of personal information.

Some information may be stored locally on your device in order to provide app functionality, such as:

- User preferences
- Profile information
- App activity and records created by you

Local data is stored on your device and is not automatically shared with other users.

## 2. Sign in with Apple

Wish may use Sign in with Apple for authentication.

When you use Sign in with Apple, Apple may provide the app with an identifier and, depending on your choices, your name or email address.

Wish does not receive your Apple ID password.

## 3. Local Data Storage

Most user-generated application data is intended to be stored locally on your device.

Deleting the app may remove locally stored data unless otherwise provided by Apple system backup mechanisms.

## 4. Third-Party Services

Certain optional features may rely on third-party services.

When such services are used, only the information necessary to provide the requested functionality will be transmitted.

This policy may be updated if additional third-party services are introduced.

## 5. Data Sharing

Wish does not sell your personal information.

We do not use your personal information for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

## 6. Children’s Privacy

Wish does not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the app or applicable requirements.

The latest version will always be available at this page.
